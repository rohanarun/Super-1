Build language models that are substantially cheaper to train and serve while approaching the quality of strong Transformer baselines. The user's current criterion permits a modest compute premium over minGRU when recurrent state is equal and quality/reliability are comparable or better. Do not reinterpret this as requiring strict compute dominance. Equally, do not call the measured 3.4× eager-CPU decode penalty a demonstrated modest GPU premium.

Compare quality against total training cost, time to a predeclared quality target, per-seed reliability, and serving cost. Keep recurrent state, model weights, peak working memory and any external tables as separate quantities. A small persistent state does not imply a small training footprint. The value of fewer failed runs belongs in the comparison, but a universal failure probability cannot be inferred from the existing small studies.

This handoff prepares the next chat; no NanoGPT training, GPU kernel validation or Zipfy experiment was run while writing it. The last inspected environment had PyTorch 2.9.1+cpu, CUDA unavailable and an AVX-512-capable CPU backend. Inspect the new environment before choosing implementation details. GPU training requires a GPU runner. The handoff itself does not provision compute or create a paid job.
